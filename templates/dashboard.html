Management Modules

Access real-time employee wellness analytical tools and predictive engines.

1. Employee Health Data Management

Collects and manages employee wellness data such as BMI, medical check-up reports, exercise habits, sleep patterns, attendance, and health assessments.

AI-based validation, pre-processing, integration

2. Wellness Risk Prediction

Predicts employees who are at risk of stress, burnout, obesity, hypertension, or other health issues based on historical and real-time data.

ML: Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, NN

3. Personalized Wellness Recommendation

Recommends customized fitness plans, diet schedules, mental wellness activities, yoga sessions, and lifestyle improvements based on individual health profiles.

Recommendation Systems, Collaborative Filtering, GenAI

4. Mental Health & Sentiment Analytics

Analyzes employee surveys, feedback, emails (with consent), or chatbot conversations to detect stress levels, anxiety, burnout, and overall employee sentiment.

NLP, Sentiment Analysis, Emotion Detection

5. Wellness Performance Dashboard

Provides HR and management with interactive dashboards showing wellness KPIs such as participation rate, absenteeism, productivity trends, health risk scores, and program effectiveness.

Predictive Analytics, Data Visualization, BI

6. AI Wellness Chatbot & Engagement

Offers 24/7 assistance by answering wellness-related questions, scheduling health checkups, sending reminders, tracking goals, and motivating employees with personalized interactions.

Conversational AI, LLMs, NLP, Reinforcement Learning
