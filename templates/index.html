About System

Employee Wellness Management

Employee Wellness Management is an AI-powered healthcare analytics platform that helps organizations monitor employee health, predict wellness risks, and provide personalized recommendations for improving employee well-being.

Project Overview

The system enables employees and administrators to make informed decisions that improve workplace health, productivity, and overall well-being. By integrating advanced health prediction models and comprehensive record tracking, the system builds a bridge between proactive care and office wellness.

Project Objectives

  • Early Health Risk Detection
  • Better Employee Wellness
  • Increased Productivity
  • Secure Health Data Management
  • AI-powered Health Insights
